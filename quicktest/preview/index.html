How each question was made

Step 1 was the research, not the writing. I listed the mistakes Korean students really make on this point, from Korean school material, then wrote 150 correct sentences with easy words over seven frames, then turned real mistakes into the wrong options. The four options in every question are four different sentences about four different things. No question repeats a sentence.

#MistakeWrongRight
1How and What swappedWhat tall she is!How tall she is!
2Missing a or anWhat big park it is!What a big park it is!
3Article after HowHow a cold day it is!How cold the day is!
4Wrong word orderHow the soup is hot!How hot the soup is!
5Question word orderHow fast does he run!How fast he runs!
6Question markWhat a cute cat it is?What a cute cat it is!
7a and an mixed upWhat a old house it is!What an old house it is!
8Article before uncountableWhat a nice weather it is!What nice weather it is!
9Article before pluralWhat a long bridges they are!What long bridges they are!
10Very inside the exclamationHow very hungry I am!How hungry I am!

A correct sentence and a wrong sentence never come from the same base, so a student cannot answer by spotting a familiar sentence.
